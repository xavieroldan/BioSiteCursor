n8n Job Scout

Automation + AI · In progress

n8n workflow that collects job offers from email alerts and RSS feeds, normalises them, removes duplicates and uses Claude to score them against a professional profile. It sends a daily Telegram digest with the best matches and logs everything to Google Sheets. Ships with versioned prompts, the exported JSON workflow and a Docker deployment guide.
