My Story

I'm Xavier Roldán, a Team Lead and senior developer with 7 years of experience in enterprise applications.

Before coding I spent over 15 years in B2B sales, automotive and IT support. That taught me to listen to clients, understand their business and commit to results.

In 2019 I trained as a backend developer (IT Academy Barcelona Activa and CIFO La Violeta) and since then I've worked at MinIT, where I lead a 3-5 person team for Athlon Spain, Athlon Belgium and Santander Leasing Sweden, and act as Scrum Master for the Mercedes Benz international team.

We've delivered 15+ projects in 2 years. My focus is CI/CD pipelines and REST APIs, working with .NET, Java, Angular and Oracle SQL.
